\subsubsection{Project Applications}

Some areas that the proposed design can be implemented in include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Increasing robustness and availability of fixed wireless connections in rural and far-flung areas 
    \item Providing stable transmission in IoT and wireless sensor networks that have directional antennas 
    \item Assisting in inexpensive point-to-point wireless connection solutions where manual adjustment of the antennas is not feasible 
    \item Being used as an experimental platform for studying embedded reinforcement learning in RF control systems 
\end{itemize}
