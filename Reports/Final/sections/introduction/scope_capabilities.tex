\subsubsection{Project Capabilities}

The project is capable of the following:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Design and implementation of mechanically-steered directional antennas through the use of servos and stepper motors controlled by a microcontroller (such as ESP32)
    \item The development of simple alignment algorithms, including full-angle scanning and RSSI hill climbing techniques, for performance comparison purposes
    \item The incorporation of a lightweight reinforcement learning system that enables changing the antenna’s angle based on the observed RSSI values
    \item Practical testing of alignment efficiency in stationary and mild dynamic scenarios
\end{itemize}
