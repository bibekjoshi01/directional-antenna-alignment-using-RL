\subsubsection{Limitations of the Project}

The project has certain limitations, which are listed below:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The project does not take into account advanced antenna configurations like MIMO or phased array systems
    \item The project does not take into account high frequency millimeter wave communications or advanced techniques for beam forming.
    \item The project considers only alignment at the receiver end without taking transmitter end alignment into account
    \item Mobility issues such as mobile transmitters or vehicular communication at high speed have not been considered
    \item Use of table based Q learning makes the solution non-scalable due to discretization of states and actions space
    \item RSSI measurement may involve errors owing to variations and randomness involved in wireless channels
\end{itemize}
